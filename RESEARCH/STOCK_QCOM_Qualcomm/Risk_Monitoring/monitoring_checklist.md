# Monitoring Checklist：Qualcomm（QCOM）

## 强化基本面信号
- 收入增长与毛利率同时改善。[StockAnalysis.com, accessed 2026-06-22, QCOM income, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/qcom/financials/, Source Quality: C]
- OCF/NI 持续健康，FCF 转正或扩大。[StockAnalysis.com, accessed 2026-06-22, QCOM cashflow, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/qcom/financials/cash-flow-statement/, Source Quality: C]
- 管理层指引与实际订单/库存相互印证。[U.S. SEC EDGAR, 2025-11-05, Qualcomm 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804328/000080432825000085/qcom-20250928.htm, Source Quality: A]
- 非核心风险（诉讼、监管、客户流失）没有恶化。

## 削弱基本面信号
- 库存和应收显著恶化。
- 毛利率下行且管理层归因不清。
- 高资本开支未带来收入/利润兑现。
- 估值扩张主要依赖叙事而非现金流改善。
