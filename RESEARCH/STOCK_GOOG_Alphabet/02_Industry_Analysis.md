- AI 可能改变搜索入口和广告展示形态。[U.S. SEC EDGAR, 2026-02-05, Alphabet 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000018/goog-20251231.htm, Source Quality: A]
