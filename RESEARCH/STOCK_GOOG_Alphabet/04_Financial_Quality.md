- OCF 与净利润的关系可验证盈利质量；FCF 受 AI/云资本开支显著影响。[StockAnalysis.com, accessed 2026-06-22, GOOG cashflow, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/goog/financials/cash-flow-statement/, Source Quality: C]
- 资产负债表快照：现金 **38,063**，总债务 **90,484**，Current Ratio **1.92**，Debt/Equity **0.20**。[StockAnalysis.com, accessed 2026-06-22, GOOG balance, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/goog/financials/balance-sheet/, Source Quality: C] [StockAnalysis.com, accessed 2026-06-22, GOOG stats, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/goog/statistics/, Source Quality: C]

## 红旗筛查
1. capex 快于收入且 FCF 下滑；2. 云/AI 毛利率恶化；3. 监管罚款或业务拆分风险；4. 股权激励稀释；5. 客户增长或续费放缓。
