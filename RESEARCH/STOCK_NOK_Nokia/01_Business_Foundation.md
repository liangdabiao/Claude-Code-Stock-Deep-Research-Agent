# 01 Business Foundation：公司事实底座

## 业务本质
全球通信设备、网络基础设施和专利授权公司，核心变量是运营商 5G/6G CAPEX 周期、Network Infrastructure/Mobile Networks 竞争力、专利授权现金流和成本结构调整。 该描述基于公司 SEC/公司披露中对业务、产品、市场和风险因素的披露。[U.S. SEC EDGAR, 2026-03-05, Nokia 20-F, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/924613/000162828026015034/nok-20251231.htm, Source Quality: A]

## 核心业务与产品线
- Mobile Networks、Network Infrastructure、Cloud and Network Services 与 Nokia Technologies 构成主要业务线。[U.S. SEC EDGAR, 2026-03-05, Nokia 20-F, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/924613/000162828026015034/nok-20251231.htm, Source Quality: A]
- Nokia Technologies 专利授权业务通常具备较高利润率和现金流韧性。[U.S. SEC EDGAR, 2026-03-05, Nokia 20-F, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/924613/000162828026015034/nok-20251231.htm, Source Quality: A]
- 设备业务受运营商 CAPEX、印度/北美需求和竞争格局影响较大。[U.S. SEC EDGAR, 2026-03-05, Nokia 20-F, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/924613/000162828026015034/nok-20251231.htm, Source Quality: A]
