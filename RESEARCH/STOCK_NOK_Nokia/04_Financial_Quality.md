- 资产负债表快照：现金 **4,951**，总债务 **3,325**，Current Ratio **1.57**，Debt/Equity **0.16**。[StockAnalysis.com, accessed 2026-06-23, NOK balance, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nok/financials/balance-sheet/, Source Quality: C] [StockAnalysis.com, accessed 2026-06-23, NOK stats, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nok/statistics/, Source Quality: C]

## 红旗筛查
1. FCF 长期为负；2. 融资稀释；3. 客户/订单突然变化；4. 监管事件；5. 大额减值或持续经营风险。
