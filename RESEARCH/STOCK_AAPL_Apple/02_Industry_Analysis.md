# 02 Industry Analysis：行业周期与竞争

## 行业周期判断
- 全球智能手机市场成熟，换机周期和高端份额是核心变量。[U.S. SEC EDGAR, 2025-10-31, Apple 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm, Source Quality: A]
- 生成式 AI 可能推动端侧设备升级但兑现节奏不确定。[U.S. SEC EDGAR, 2025-10-31, Apple 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm, Source Quality: A]
- 监管对 App Store 佣金、支付和生态封闭性持续施压。[U.S. SEC EDGAR, 2025-10-31, Apple 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm, Source Quality: A]

## 竞争格局
- 可比公司：Samsung Electronics、Microsoft、Google、Xiaomi、Sony。
- 竞争变量包括云资本开支、AI 产品化、企业软件续费、平台监管、开发者生态和广告/硬件周期。[U.S. SEC EDGAR, 2025-10-31, Apple 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm, Source Quality: A]

## 外部变量
- 大型科技平台普遍受监管、数据安全、AI 模型安全、知识产权、供应链和资本开支周期影响。[U.S. SEC EDGAR, 2025-10-31, Apple 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm, Source Quality: A]

## 核心监控数据
1. 云增速与 backlog/RPO；2. AI 产品 ARPU/采用率；3. capex 与 FCF；4. 监管案件；5. 毛利率和经营杠杆。
