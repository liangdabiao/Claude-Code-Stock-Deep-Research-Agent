# 06 Market Sentiment：市场分歧

## Bull Case
- 品牌、生态和用户粘性极强。
- Services 高毛利提高盈利韧性。
- 强 FCF 和回购能力支持股东回报。

## Bear Case
- 硬件成长成熟化。
- 中国和监管风险持续。
- 估值对低增长环境较敏感。

## 争议如何验证
- 财务验证：收入增长、毛利率、OCF/NI、FCF 与 capex。[StockAnalysis.com, accessed 2026-06-22, AAPL income, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aapl/financials/, Source Quality: C] [StockAnalysis.com, accessed 2026-06-22, AAPL cashflow, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aapl/financials/cash-flow-statement/, Source Quality: C]
- 业务验证：管理层关于云/AI/广告/硬件需求和客户合同的披露。[U.S. SEC EDGAR, 2025-10-31, Apple 10-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019325000079/aapl-20250927.htm, Source Quality: A]
- 估值验证：PE、Forward PE、PS、EV/EBITDA 是否已反映乐观情形。[StockAnalysis.com, accessed 2026-06-22, AAPL stats, https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/aapl/statistics/, Source Quality: C]

## 关键观察节点
季度业绩、云增速、AI 产品商业化、监管案件、capex 指引、股东回报。
